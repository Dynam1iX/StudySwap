# Final test — StudySwap (Final Project, A4)

Tester (team member): Alzhan Yerzhanuly
Date: 2026-11-08
System: https://studyswap-phi.vercel.app (final version, after PR #58)
Participants: AITU students who are **not** members of the team (no names — P1…P4)

## Method
Each participant signed in with their own AITU e-mail on their own phone. The tester gave each task, started a stopwatch at the first touch of the screen and stopped it when the task was done. No help was given during a task.

- **SC1** — find 3 possible swap partners for a subject the participant needs. Target: ≤ 2 min.
- **SC2** — create and publish a barter card (AI check included). Target: < 3 min.
- **SC3** — the whole journey works on a narrow phone screen without horizontal scrolling or cut-off buttons.
